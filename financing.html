Used car financing in Dorval

Approval, rate and terms depend on the lender and the application. Here is how to prepare and compare an offer.

A monthly payment is only one part of a financing offer. Compare the amount borrowed, the credit rate, the term, the fees and the total obligation before deciding.

Each lender assesses the complete application

Automobile SX works with several Canadian lenders. Their criteria differ, and the lender—not the dealership—decides whether to approve an application and which rate and terms to offer.

  • Credit history is one factor. There is no score on this site that promises approval or a particular rate.
  • Income and existing obligations matter. The lender must assess whether the requested credit can be repaid.
  • The vehicle and term matter. Eligibility can vary with the amount requested and the vehicle selected.
  • A down payment is application-specific. Do not assume one is required—or that a particular amount will change the result—until it appears in a written offer.

Confirm the documents before applying

A lender may ask for documents that verify identity, address, income, banking details or a trade-in. The exact list depends on the application. Ask what is required before sending personal information. Examples can include:

  • Government-issued identification and proof of address
  • Documents supporting income or employment
  • Banking information requested for the application
  • Trade-in registration and a current payout statement, if applicable

Compare the complete written offer

Rates change and an advertised range cannot predict an individual approval. Ask for the rate, payment schedule, financing fees, amount financed, term and total obligation in writing. The Financial Consumer Agency of Canada recommends comparing the total cost rather than only the payment or interest rate.

Understand the effect of the term

A longer term can lower each payment while increasing the interest paid over the life of the loan. It can also increase the time you owe more than the vehicle is worth. Compare the same amount and rate over different terms before choosing.

Your rights in Quebec, in plain language

  • Financing cannot be forced on you. Quebec prohibits making a road-vehicle purchase conditional on dealer-arranged credit or long-term leasing. Read the OPC summary.
  • An instalment-sale contract must disclose the complete figures. That includes the cash price, down payment if any, credit charges, credit rate, total obligation, term and payments. See the OPC contract checklist.
  • Early repayment and cancellation rules depend on the contract. An instalment-sale contract must allow early payment without fees or penalty and normally carries a two-day cancellation right; high-cost credit has different rules. Check the OPC cancellation guidance.
