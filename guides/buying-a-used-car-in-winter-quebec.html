Buying a used car in winter in Quebec: Can you drive it home without winter tires?

Quebec's winter tire deadline, the seven-day exemption and how to plan pickup.

By Automobile SX · Published November 11, 2026

Quebec generally requires winter tires from December 1 through March 15. If you're picking up a used car during that period, plan to have compliant tires installed before you drive home. If you bought from a dealer, Quebec allows a seven-day exception after you acquire the car, provided you carry the bill of sale or a copy. A dealer's 10-day transit has its own registration deadline, and the winter tire exception tied to that document is capped at seven days. Quebec winter tire rules | SAAQ temporary registration

Plan your pickup around road conditions

If snow, freezing rain or icy roads are in the forecast, it's worth getting winter tires installed before pickup, even in November or while a legal exemption still applies. Black ice can be hard to see, even when the pavement looks clear. Check the forecast and Quebec 511 for your route before leaving. If conditions are poor, call the seller to arrange installation before you drive home or agree on a better pickup time.

Which rule applies to your purchase?

Your situationWhat to check before driving
You bought from a dealerThe seven-day winter tire exception runs from the acquisition date. Carry the bill of sale or a copy.
The dealer issued a temporary registration certificate (transit)The dealer transit can be valid for 10 days for registration. If the transit is displayed on the vehicle, the winter tire exception linked to it ends when the transit expires or seven days after issuance, whichever comes first.
The car is registered and you're waiting for a metal plateThe paper licence plate lets you drive while you wait for the metal one. Check whether a separate winter tire exception applies to your situation.
You bought from a private sellerIf you need the winter tire exception, request an exemption certificate through SAAQclic or at a SAAQ service outlet. The seven-day period starts on the acquisition date.

What if you buy in November?

Say you acquire a used car from a dealer and get a 10-day transit on November 23. On December 1, more than seven days have passed since both the acquisition and the transit's issuance. The transit may still be valid for registration purposes, but neither of those two winter tire exceptions still applies. If you plan to drive on December 1, arrange installation ahead of time unless a different exemption applies. Quebec winter tire rules | SAAQ temporary registration

Ask these four questions before pickup

  1. Which tires will be mounted when I pick up the car? Ask whether a winter set is already installed or comes separately. Look for the mountain-and-snowflake symbol on the sidewall. Studded tires are also recognized under Quebec's rules.
  2. What exactly is included in the written price? If winter tires are part of the deal, ask the seller to put the set and any agreed wheels or installation in writing.
  3. How much tread is left and how old are the tires? Ask for the tread depth on all four tires and the date codes. If you're unsure about a used set, ask a tire shop to check it.
  4. Can the winter tires be installed before pickup? If the car is on summer or ordinary all-season tires, confirm who will arrange the installation and when.
